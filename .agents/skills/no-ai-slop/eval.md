# Self-check before returning an edited draft

Go through the edited draft against every line below. If any box would fail, fix the draft and check again before returning it.

## Voice preserved

- [ ] The draft still sounds like the same person who wrote it — vocabulary, cadence, bluntness, humor, uncertainty, and digressions the writer used on purpose are intact.
- [ ] No paragraph was smoothed into generic polish just for consistency.
- [ ] No claim, example, stat, or opinion was invented or altered in meaning.
- [ ] Strong human sentences were left alone rather than rewritten for symmetry.

## Banned words gone

- [ ] None of: delve, foster, leverage, utilize, facilitate, empower, streamline, robust, cutting-edge, paradigm shift, game changer, this is huge, this changes everything, tapestry, realm, beacon, multifaceted, meticulous, intricate, paramount, transformative, elevate, embark, supercharge, harness, ever-evolving.
- [ ] Empty adverbs (just, literally, honestly, simply, actually, truly, fundamentally, importantly, crucially, inherently, inevitably) are either cut or clearly earning their place (real emphasis, uncertainty, contrast, or the writer's spoken rhythm).
- [ ] Empty phrases (it's worth noting, at the end of the day, when it comes to, at its core, in today's world, the reality is, in terms of, going forward, let's dive in, etc.) are cut unless one is a recognizable part of the writer's voice.

## Patterns gone

- [ ] No binary contrasts ("It's not X, it's Y" / "not just X but Y").
- [ ] No throat-clearing openers ("Here's the thing," "Let me be clear," "I'll be honest").
- [ ] No faux-insight setups ("What most people get wrong," "Here's what nobody tells you").
- [ ] No colon reveals (noun phrase, colon, lowercase dramatic payoff).
- [ ] No superficial trailing `-ing` analysis clauses (highlighting, underscoring, reflecting, showcasing) standing in for a real mechanism or consequence.
- [ ] No importance puffery ("stands as a testament," "marks a pivotal moment," "underscores its significance").
- [ ] No interpretive metadiscourse telling the reader what to notice or how to weigh it ("That matters more than it sounds," "As you can see," redundant "in other words").
- [ ] No weasel attribution ("experts agree," "studies show") without a named source.
- [ ] No fake-strong verb phrases where "is"/"has" would be clearer ("serves as," "plays a role in").
- [ ] No synonym cycling — the same clear word is repeated instead of rotated for style.
- [ ] No negative listing ("Not a X. Not a Y. A Z.").
- [ ] No dramatic fragmentation ("X. And Y. And Z." / "That's it. That's the whole thing.").
- [ ] No robotic rhythm — sentence and paragraph shapes vary, not stacked identically.
- [ ] No rhetorical setups ("What if I told you," "Plot twist:", self-answered question/answer pairs).
- [ ] No fake-profound kicker line turning the ending into a metaphor or mic-drop.
- [ ] No summary-recap ending ("In conclusion," "Ultimately," a paragraph that just restates the piece).
- [ ] No formatting slop — emoji in headings, mid-sentence bold for emphasis, bullets where prose reads better, headers over two-sentence sections.
- [ ] Em dashes: none in short copy; at most 1-2 in longer drafts, only where they clearly beat a comma, period, or parenthesis; no clusters.

## Concreteness

- [ ] Every claim that could be made more specific (names, numbers, dates, mechanisms, examples) has been, where the user's material supports it.
- [ ] Any sentence that would still be true if you swapped in a different person/company/product (the portability test) was cut or replaced with something specific to this subject.
- [ ] Facts and examples carry the emphasis; commentary that just labels something "important" or "surprising" instead of showing why has been cut.

## Mechanics

- [ ] Active voice used where it's clearer; inanimate things aren't doing human verbs.
- [ ] Weak verb phrases replaced with direct verbs ("made a decision" → "decided").
- [ ] Sentences that were genuinely hard to follow were split; sentences that were long but clear and characteristic were left alone.
- [ ] The user's structure and progression were kept unless reorganizing genuinely helped — and if reorganized, the What changed section says why.
